About VXM

A platform built around earning opportunities

VXM is a digital earning platform designed to give users access to structured daily tasks, earning opportunities, referral rewards and a personal wallet experience.

Our Approach

Making digital earning easier to understand

Transparent rules

VXM aims to create a platform where users can clearly understand their available tasks, track their activity and manage their account from one place.

Clear dashboards

Rather than hiding important information, the platform is designed around clear dashboards, transparent task information, wallet tracking and defined platform rules.

Trackable progress

Monitor daily activity, keep track of balances, and understand platform terms from your personal account.

Platform Features

Everything you need on your earning journey

Daily Tasks

Access available daily reel and advertising tasks through your account. Task availability depends on your active level.

Referral Opportunities

Invite eligible users using your unique referral code or link. Referral rewards follow the configured VXM rules.

M-Pesa Withdrawals

Manage eligible withdrawal requests through the M-Pesa integration. Status updates appear in your account.

Wallet Tracking

View available, pending and historical account balances in one place with a full transaction ledger.

Task History

Keep track of completed tasks and remaining daily activity so you always know where you stand.

Support

Get assistance with accounts, payments, tasks, withdrawals and referrals through the in-platform ticket system.
