How referrals work

The referral journey is organized so you can understand what happens at each stage.

02

03

Track activity

What you get

Referral features

Unique link & code

Each account can receive its own referral link and code after registration.

Share freely

Share with eligible people who may join the platform through your preferred channels.

Activity tracking

View referral counts and activity from your account area when data is available.

Rule-based rewards

Rewards follow official VXM rules when a referral qualifies. Handled by VXM according to official terms.

Eligibility rules

Referrals must meet configured platform rules. Availability, reward amounts and eligibility depend on the official configuration.

No guaranteed returns

Values such as “XX referrals” or “KES XXX” on marketing pages are placeholders only. They are not financial promises or guaranteed earnings.
