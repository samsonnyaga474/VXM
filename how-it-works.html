How It Works

From registration to withdrawal

VXM organizes the earning journey into clear, manageable steps so you always know what to do next.

01 — Create Your Account

Register

Register your VXM account and create your personal platform profile using your basic information and a secure password. You can also use a referral link if you were invited.

02 — Choose a Level

Select an earning level

Select an available earning level according to the platform's configured rules. Your level determines which tasks you can access and the reward structure that applies to you.

03 — Complete Tasks

Daily activity

Access eligible daily reel and digital tasks through your account. Complete them according to the instructions. Eligible rewards are recorded in your wallet.

04 — Track & Withdraw

Manage your earnings

Monitor eligible earnings in your wallet and manage withdrawal requests. Withdrawals follow the platform rules and may go through M-Pesa once approved.

Good to know

Important platform points

Level rules apply

Task access and reward amounts depend on the level you have selected. Exact prices and rewards are set by the platform administrator.

Daily eligibility

Some tasks are available once per day. Your dashboard shows which tasks remain available for the current period.

Transparent ledger

Every credit and debit is recorded in the transaction ledger so you can always review the history of your balance.
