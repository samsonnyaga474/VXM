Earning Levels

Select an available VXM level

VXM levels are designed to organize available tasks, rewards and referral opportunities according to the platform's configured rules. Before selecting a level, review the available tasks, joining requirements and applicable rewards.

Level 01

Entry

KES —

Starter access to daily tasks. Exact price, task list and rewards are configured by the platform administrator.

  • Access to entry-level daily tasks
  • Wallet and transaction history
  • Referral participation
  • Support tickets

Level 02

Growth

KES —

Expanded task access and higher reward potential under the configured rules.

  • Higher task rewards
  • More daily task slots
  • Stronger referral benefits
  • Full wallet features
Get Started
Level 03

Pro

KES —

Maximum task access and earning capacity available under current platform rules.

  • Full task catalogue
  • Highest configured rewards
  • Premium referral rates
  • Priority support options

Current prices: Starter KES 500, Growth KES 1,500, Pro KES 3,500. Referral reward is 5% of a qualifying level purchase. Logged-in users select a level from the dashboard after depositing funds. These are configurable settings, not guaranteed investment returns.
