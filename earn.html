How you earn on VXM

Eligible users complete daily tasks, grow through referrals, and manage rewards in a personal wallet under the platform rules.

Daily Tasks

Eligible users can access their available daily tasks through the VXM platform. The dashboard shows which tasks have been completed, which remain available, and the reward associated with each eligible task.

Start earning

Referrals

VXM includes a referral system that allows users to invite eligible new users using a unique referral link or code. Referral rewards are calculated according to the configured VXM rules.

Get your link

Earning Levels

Your selected level determines task access and the reward structure that applies to you. Level prices and benefits are configured by the platform administrator and shown on the levels page.

View levels
Wallet

Your earnings stay visible

Every eligible reward is recorded in your wallet with a transaction history you can review at any time.

Balance tracking

View available balance, pending amounts, and historical activity from your dashboard.

Transaction ledger

Credits from tasks and referrals, and debits from withdrawals, are all logged for transparency.

Withdrawals

Request withdrawals according to platform rules. Eligible requests can be processed via M-Pesa after admin review where required.
