Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Refund Policy describes how refunds are handled on the VXM platform.

Important: Exact refund eligibility, windows, and processing times are business decisions that must be confirmed by the platform owner and, where appropriate, by legal counsel. The sections below use clear placeholders for those decisions.

1. Level purchases / deposits used to join a level

Once a level has been activated and the user has begun using level features (including tasks), the amount applied to that level is generally non-refundable, except where required by applicable law or where the platform owner has approved an exception. 2. Failed or incomplete M-Pesa deposits

If a deposit is charged by the payment provider but does not credit the VXM wallet because of a verified technical failure, VXM will investigate and, where the payment is confirmed and not already credited, work to resolve the issue (credit the wallet or facilitate a refund through the original payment channel). Processing times depend on the payment provider.

3. Task rewards and referral bonuses

Task rewards, XP, and referral bonuses that have been correctly calculated and credited according to platform rules are not “refunds” in the ordinary sense; they form part of the platform’s reward system. Disputes about calculation should be raised through support with supporting details.

4. Withdrawals

Once a withdrawal has been approved and sent via M-Pesa, it cannot be reversed by VXM. Pending withdrawal requests may be cancelled by the user or rejected by administrators according to platform rules before processing.

5. How to request a review

Contact support through the in-app support form or [INSERT VERIFIED SUPPORT EMAIL], providing the transaction reference, approximate time, amount, and a clear description of the issue. We aim to respond within [INSERT TARGET RESPONSE TIME, e.g. 3–5 business days].

6. Chargebacks and payment provider disputes

If you open a dispute with your payment provider, VXM may suspend related account activity while the dispute is investigated, in line with the Terms of Service and applicable rules.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated. The “Last updated” date will be revised when changes are published.
