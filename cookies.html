Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookies Policy explains how VXM uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What we use

VXM uses essential cookies and similar technologies required for the platform to function:

  • Session cookie — keeps you logged in securely after authentication.
  • CSRF token — helps protect forms against cross-site request forgery.
  • Other strictly necessary technical identifiers for security and load balancing if used by the hosting environment.

2. Third-party resources currently loaded

Public pages load fonts from Google Fonts and icons from the Bootstrap Icons CDN. Those providers may receive your IP address and basic browser data when the page loads. They are used for presentation, not advertising.

3. What we do not currently use

At the time of writing, VXM does not load third-party advertising pixels, Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, TikTok Pixel, Hotjar, Microsoft Clarity, or similar non-essential tracking scripts. If this changes, this policy and the Privacy Policy will be updated and consent mechanisms will be reviewed.

3. Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies will prevent login and core platform features from working correctly.

4. Consent

Because only essential cookies are used for core functionality, a marketing-style cookie consent popup is not currently required for the technologies described above. If non-essential tracking is introduced later, an appropriate consent mechanism will be implemented.
