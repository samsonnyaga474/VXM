Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VXM collects, uses, and protects information when you use the platform.

1. Information we collect

Account information (name, email, phone), authentication data, wallet and transaction records, task activity, referral relationships, support messages, and technical logs necessary to operate and secure the service.

2. How we use information

To provide the platform, process deposits and withdrawals, calculate eligible rewards, prevent fraud, respond to support requests, and improve reliability and security.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with payment processors (such as M-Pesa providers) as required to complete transactions, and with service providers under confidentiality obligations. We may disclose information when required by law.

4. Security

We use industry-standard measures including password hashing, CSRF protection, secure sessions, and access controls. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

5. Retention

We retain account and transaction data for as long as needed to operate the platform, meet legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

6. Your choices

You may update account details from your profile, request support regarding your data, and close your account subject to outstanding obligations and platform rules.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact form or open a support ticket from your account.

This is a baseline privacy document. Replace or expand with final legal text provided by your counsel before production launch.
