Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how VXM (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data when you use the VXM platform.

1. Data controller

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: full name, email, phone number, password (hashed), referral code.
  • Financial data: wallet balances, deposits, withdrawals, M-Pesa transaction references, earnings history.
  • Activity data: selected level, task completions, XP points, referral relationships, support tickets.
  • Technical data: session identifiers, IP address (where logged for security), browser/device information, and timestamps.
  • Communications: messages you send through support or contact forms.

We only collect data that is necessary to operate the platform, process payments, prevent fraud, and provide support.

3. Why we use the information

To create and manage accounts, process deposits and withdrawals via M-Pesa, calculate and record task rewards and XP, enforce level rules, detect abuse, respond to support requests, and meet legal obligations. We do not use personal data for advertising profiling unless you are clearly informed and a lawful basis is established.

4. Legal basis (Kenya)

Where the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 applies, processing is based on one or more of: performance of a contract with you, your consent (where required), legitimate interests that do not override your rights, and legal obligations. This statement is not a certification of compliance.

5. Sharing and third parties

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as needed with payment processors (M-Pesa / Safaricom infrastructure), hosting providers, and service providers under appropriate terms. Public pages currently load Google Fonts and Bootstrap Icons from third-party CDNs; those providers may receive IP address and browser data. We may disclose information when required by law or to protect rights and safety.

6. Cookies and similar technologies

VXM uses essential session cookies and similar technologies required for login, security (CSRF), and basic operation. We do not currently operate third-party advertising pixels or non-essential analytics trackers. See the Cookies Policy for details.

7. Security

We use password hashing, CSRF protection, secure session configuration, prepared SQL statements, and access controls. No system is completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.

8. Retention

Account, transaction, and task history are retained while the account is active and for a reasonable period afterward to meet legal, accounting, and dispute-resolution needs. Exact retention periods should be confirmed with counsel.

9. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object or withdraw consent where consent is the basis. Contact us using the details above. You may also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner (Kenya) where applicable.

10. Children

The platform is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be indicated by updating the “Last updated” date and, where appropriate, notifying users through the platform.

12. Contact
